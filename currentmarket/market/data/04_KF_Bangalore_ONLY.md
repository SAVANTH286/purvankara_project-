# Knight Frank — Bengaluru ONLY extract
Source PDF: sources/KF_India_RE_H2_2025.pdf  
Report: India Real Estate — Office & Residential Market H2 2025  
Filter rule: use only Bengaluru residential content below for DSS city learning.

## Headline numbers (Bengaluru residential)
- H2 2025 launches: 35,262 (highest H2 ever)
- 2025 launches: +23% YoY vs prior year; sales muted / roughly flat
- Unsold inventory: 67,518 units (+25% YoY)
- Quarters to sell (QTS): ~4.9 overall
- Sub-INR 5 mn inventory QTS: ~20.3 (stress)
- INR 10–50 mn segment QTS: ~2.9–3.1 (healthy)
- Weighted average capital value: +12% YoY
- Average unit size launched: +7% YoY (larger homes)

## Zone split H2 2025
- North: ~34% launches / ~33% sales (overtook East)
- South: ~34% launches / ~32% sales
- East: ~27% launches / ~30% sales

## Hot pockets called out
- North: Hebbal, Thanisandra, Jakkur, Airport Road
- South transformation: Begur, Sarjapur Road, Electronic City (Yellow Line)
- East: still powerful; relative share diluted by North rise; future spillover to Hoskote / Budigere Cross
