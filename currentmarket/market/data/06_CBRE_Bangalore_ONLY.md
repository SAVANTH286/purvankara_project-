## Bengaluru signals from CBRE

### 1) Affordability / buyer cohorts (Outlook 2026)
CBRE Housing Affordability Index covers **Bengaluru** among 6 cities.

Ticket-size definitions for **Bengaluru & Hyderabad** (CBRE):
- Affordable: up to ₹45 lakh
- Budget: ₹45–75 lakh
- Mid-end: ₹75 lakh–1.5 crore
- High-end: ₹1.5–2.5 crore
- Premium: ₹2.5–5 crore
- Luxury: ₹5–50 crore
- Ultra Luxury: ₹50 crore+

Bengaluru micro-market examples cited in affordability commentary:
- **Bengaluru North** — INR 40 lakh HH income cohort typically targeting ~2BHK; EMI burden expected to plateau; homes in ~₹1.25–2 crore range become more reachable
- **Bengaluru East and West** — INR 75 lakh cohort targeting ~3BHK; affordability moving from “moderate” to “attainable”

### 2) National framing that applies to Bengaluru positioning
From Outlook 2026 / press:
- Flight-to-quality / high-end share rising
- 2025: national launches & sales each >270,000 units
- High-end ~27% of national sales (overtook mid-end)
- Premium/luxury sales +30% YoY nationally
- Sales volume −8% but sales value +15% → quality/ticket-size shift
- Affordability expected to **stabilise 2026–2028** as income growth outpaces price growth

### 3) Market Monitor Q1 2026 (page summary)
- Bengaluru named among cities contributing a **substantial share of new project launches** (with Mumbai & Hyderabad)
- High-end and luxury housing continued to gain traction
